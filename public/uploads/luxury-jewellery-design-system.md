# Luxury Jewellery E-Commerce Website Design System

## Brand Direction

Design a modern luxury jewellery e-commerce website inspired by premium brands like Daisy London, Mejuri, Cartier modern collections, and Indian luxury editorial aesthetics.

The website should feel:
- trustworthy
- elegant
- premium
- minimal
- emotionally warm
- feminine yet modern
- clean and editorial

The design must prioritize:
1. Product imagery
2. Trust-building
3. Emotional luxury
4. Calm browsing experience
5. Subtle premium interactions

Avoid:
- overly flashy animations
- cluttered layouts
- aggressive gradients
- trendy glassmorphism
- excessive shadows
- loud typography

The entire experience should feel breathable and refined.

---

# Brand Personality

The brand personality is:
- elegant
- timeless
- soft luxury
- emotionally warm
- sophisticated
- modern Indian luxury
- trusted craftsmanship

Users should feel:
- safe purchasing expensive jewellery
- emotionally connected to products
- calm while browsing
- encouraged to explore slowly

Use:

- #111b43
- white text

Gold tones should be used carefully:
- borders
- hover accents
